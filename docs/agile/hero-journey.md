# Hero journey

One story runs through all four ownership areas. It is the spine of the W5 proposal and the W18 final presentation, and Playwright automates it as **E2E #1**. Two proof points sit beside it: another company joining without code changes (**E2E #2**), and HQ being refused fields it may not hold (**E2E #3**).

Adopted from Azri's integrated plan (v3), and moved to statement #7 on 22 October 2026 ([ADR 0022](../adr/0022-final-statement-7-lesso-solar.md)): the journey is now the brief's suggested flow, onboarding a new hire into both INFO-TECH and SAP from one entry, with the SAP record in place before intranet access. It uses the personas in [personas.md](../design-thinking/personas.md) and follows the future-state journey maps in [journey-maps.md](../design-thinking/journey-maps.md). Everything in it is synthetic demo data from mocked systems, and every write goes to a mock, labelled as one.

## Automated scenarios

Each scenario is a Playwright spec in `apps/web/e2e/` that runs on every pull request with axe checks ([test plan](test-plan.md#test-levels)). Each step is a named `test.step` that carries its owning area, so a failure points to an owner. A step whose story is not done yet is marked `fixme` with the story key, and is switched on in the pull request that delivers the story ([P-9](backlog.md#p-9-hero-journey-and-proof-points-as-end-to-end-tests)).

## Presenter plan (W5 proposal and W18 final)

Each area presents its own steps, so every member demonstrates code they wrote. The backup presenter is the area's backup owner ([module-ownership.md](../team/module-ownership.md#backup-owners)). Timings are set once the lecturer confirms the length of the slot.

| Segment                   | Presenter       | What they show                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Opening and impact        | All             | Today's re-entry across INFO-TECH, SAP and the intranet, the problem, and the three measured outcomes ([measurement protocol](../design-thinking/success-metrics.md#measurement-protocol)) |
| Onboarding                | Area 2 owner    | Steps 1 to 4: the approval arrives once, one entry, the SAP and INFO-TECH writes approved by their stewards, and intranet access only after the SAP record                                 |
| Employee master and AI    | Area 1 (Richie) | Steps 5 and 6: activation from the verified INFO-TECH record and reconciliation with SAP; then E2E #2, another company's export mapped by the AI and approved by HR                        |
| Claims and Treasury       | Area 2 owner    | Step 7: the claim above the threshold, routed to HQ Treasury and not payable before its approval                                                                                           |
| Single view and leave     | Area 3 owner    | Steps 8 and 9: the single view's dashboards, the leave mapping, the exceptions and HQ's summary                                                                                            |
| Governance and monitoring | Area 4 owner    | E2E #3, the field HQ cannot get, with its audit trail; then the observability, clickstream and AI-cost dashboards                                                                          |
| Closing                   | All             | The evidence, the privacy boundaries, how another company joins, and the limits of a PoC on synthetic data                                                                                 |

At the W5 proposal the same spine is walked through on the prototype, and each member presents their own area ([T-5](backlog.md#t-5-proposal-presentation-w5-25)).

## Rules for the demo

- **Honest labels.** Every mocked system shows a "Mock" badge, and suggestions from the deterministic mock AI say so. A mock is never presented as a live integration.
- **Writes go to mocks.** Every write the journey shows lands in a mock, labelled as a write-back to a mock; writes to the client's real systems wait for the client's approval ([Q-17](../client/questions-for-client.md)).
- **Synthetic data only**, labelled as such on every screen.
- **Rehearsed with a fallback.** A one-click reset, a timed rehearsal, a recorded backup demo and a tested Docker fallback are part of [P-8](backlog.md#p-8-hardening-and-handover).
