## Handling confidential material

- **Structure, not people.** We ask for headers, code lists, blank templates and dummy rows. Please do not send real employee data. If real personal data reaches us by mistake, we stop using it, delete it and tell you the same day.
- **Where material lives.** Documents you share go to a private team folder that only the four team members and our NYP lecturers can open. This file records only answers you are comfortable with us keeping in the team's private repository, and the repository stays private while it holds any client material.
- **No onward sharing.** Material you mark confidential never appears in public slides, posts or demos, and we do not paste your documents into external services, including AI services, without your written approval.
- **Naming.** Every deliverable calls you "Organisation E" unless you agree otherwise (Q-53).
- **Minutes.** We send minutes for your corrections within two working days, and we record a meeting only with your consent.
- **End of project.** When the project ends, we delete or return your material unless you ask us to keep it.
