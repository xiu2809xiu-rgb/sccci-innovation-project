# ADR 0023: Retarget to Problem Statement #5, ASPRI-IPI

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-24
- **Traceability:** M1 (design thinking) · M2 (GRS by design) · capabilities A to G

## Context

On 23 Oct 2026 LESSO Solar withdrew from this round, so statement #7 ([ADR 0022](0022-final-statement-7-lesso-solar.md)) is gone. The team resubmitted its choices with **#5 ASPRI-IPI** first, then #4 CLA Asia and #11 Spectra Innovations. The final allocation is announced on 26 Oct 2026. The team expects #5 and starts now; until the allocation is announced, #5 is the team's first choice, not its allocation. Prompt 005 ([`../ai/prompts/005-retarget-ps5-aspri-ipi.md`](../ai/prompts/005-retarget-ps5-aspri-ipi.md)) sets out the changes.

ASPRI-IPI is the training arm of the Association of Process Industry. Its assessors observe practical assessments in real training environments, tick paper checklists, work out each trainee's competency by hand and re-type the results into the Training Management System (TMS). Operations staff check the TMS against the paper and submit the results to MOM (AMS), SSG/WSDA and ITE (STEP), some of which have no API. The brief asks for tablet-based checklists, competency calculated from each course's rules, the TMS as the single source of truth, and submissions through an agency's API or, where there is none, an agency-specific file ([problem-statement.md](../problem-statement.md)).

Of the three choices, #5 is the closest to what is built: data entered once and carried into a system of record, with files where a system has no API. The question is what carries over, what changes, and in what order.

## Decision

We will retarget the project to statement #5 by editing what exists, not by rebuilding it:

## Consequences

- **Positive.** The platform built so far is reused almost whole, and the brief's priorities (capture, competency rules and the TMS output) map onto patterns that exist: definition-driven workflows, the mapping engine, preview before any write, and the field allowlist.
- **Negative.** Most domain content needs rework: the employee master, the HR mocks and seed data, the HR screens, the screenshots and the deck. For several pull requests the documents describe #5 while the code still shows the HR domain.
- **Neutral.** The allocation is not confirmed until 26 Oct. The archive tag makes stopping cheap if it is not #5: nothing of #7 is lost, and the reframe so far is documents.
