# ADR 0022: The final statement, #7 LESSO Solar

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Traceability:** M1 (design thinking) · M2 (GRS by design) · capabilities A to G

## Context

The team built its first analysis and prototype on Problem Statement #17: Organisation E, an anonymised financial-services group with an OA platform, a Singapore HR system, HQ's ERP and SAP, and an Excel master as the only consolidated view. On 22 Oct 2026 the team was allocated the renumbered statement **#7, LESSO Solar**, which replaces #17. Prompt 003 ([`../ai/prompts/003-lessosolar-statement.md`](../ai/prompts/003-lessosolar-statement.md)) sets out the changes.

LESSO Solar is the solar division of LESSO Group, with a regional hub in Singapore. Singapore runs INFO-TECH for employee records, leave and payroll; China HQ runs a customised SAP and an intranet for news, policies, claims and approvals. The brief adds two rules: a new employee is created in HQ's SAP before they get intranet access, and some payments need an extra HQ Treasury approval. It asks for integrated or consolidated data, a single view with dashboards and insights, less duplicate entry, and reuse by other Chinese companies in Singapore ([problem-statement.md](../problem-statement.md)).

Both statements describe the same pattern, so the architecture, the stack, the four ownership areas and what is built all stay. The question is what to change, and how much.

## Decision

We will retarget the project to statement #7 by editing what exists, not by rebuilding it:

| Topic                 | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The basis             | Statement #7 is the problem we solve. The #17 analysis stays as a short superseded appendix to the problem statement, with the full text in the history.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| The systems           | `mock-sg-hr` becomes an INFO-TECH-like mock with INFO-TECH-style exports; `mock-cn-hq` stays the customised SAP mock; `mock-oa` becomes the HQ intranet mock (news, policies, claims and approvals, with signed webhooks). **The technical ids stay** (`mock-sg-hr`, `mock-cn-hq`, `mock-oa`, and the connector ids `sg-hr`, `cn-hq` and `oa`): renaming them would touch the compose file, CI, seeds, tests and every document for no change in behaviour, and an intranet's approval module is what many Chinese companies call OA (办公自动化). What users see says INFO-TECH, SAP and the HQ intranet. |
| Messaging             | A Lark/Feishu notification adapter joins DingTalk, both mocked and labelled, as the brief's suggested extension. Microsoft Teams came from #17's text and leaves the default connector set until the briefing call says what the Singapore office uses (Q-61).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| The hero journey      | Onboarding a new hire into both INFO-TECH and SAP from one entry, with intranet access granted only after the SAP record exists, replaces the OA manpower request that started onboarding. The draft-to-active rule stays. Writes to the mocks may be demonstrated, labelled; writes to the client's real systems stay out of scope until the client approves them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Claims and payments   | A claim or payment above a configurable threshold needs an extra HQ Treasury approval step. It belongs to area 2.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| The areas             | The four areas and their owners stay. Area 2 becomes onboarding and approval workflows; area 3 becomes the single view, dashboards and insights, with leave consolidation as one part of it. Areas 1 and 4 keep their scope, with area 1's connectors for INFO-TECH, SAP, the intranet and Lark/DingTalk.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Reuse                 | "Another Chinese company in Singapore joins" replaces "a new subsidiary of the same group". A small tenant configuration (company name, connector set, field mappings and theme tokens) makes reuse concrete. No LESSO theme until the client allows it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Regulation            | The MAS Technology Risk Management Guidelines and FEAT principles applied only to a financial-services group and drop out. PDPA and PIPL stay, with the CAC's 2024 cross-border provisions and China's data and cybersecurity laws.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Data and brand        | The data stays synthetic, and the brief and any client sample exports are never committed. A git-ignored `samples/` folder holds real exports when they arrive. The synthetic organisation (entity names, departments and job titles) moves to a solar company's context. The demo accounts' email domain, `orge.example`, stays for now: changing it would re-capture every screenshot and touch every sign-in test, and putting the client's name on fabricated people should wait until the client agrees to the use of its brand (Q-62).                                                               |
| The order of the work | Pull requests in this order: the brief and its analysis; the areas and the hero journey; the mocks and data; the tenant configuration; the backlog; then the slides and diagrams, re-captured from the changed product.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

## Consequences

- **Positive.** The analysis and the demo rest on a named client and its real systems; the hero flow is the one the brief suggests; the briefing call can confirm specifics instead of the basics.
- **Negative.** Documents, mocks, seed data, screenshots and the deck all need rework, and for a few pull requests the documents describe the new statement while the data still shows the old organisation's departments.
- **Neutral.** The technical ids keep their #17-era names (`mock-oa`, `orge.example`). Each is recorded here so that a reader who finds them knows why; the team lead may rename them once the briefing call settles the systems and the brand.
