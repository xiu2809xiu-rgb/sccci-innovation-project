# ADR 0024: The final statement, #11 Spectra Innovations

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-27
- **Traceability:** M1 (design thinking) · M2 (GRS by design) · capabilities A to G

## Context

The allocation was announced in W2, on 26 Oct 2026: the team has **Problem Statement #11, Spectra Innovations**, and the allocation is final. Statement #5 (ASPRI-IPI), the team's resubmitted first choice, was not allocated, so prompt 005 ([ADR 0023](0023-retarget-ps5-aspri-ipi.md)) stopped where it was. Prompt 006 ([`../ai/prompts/006-final-ps11-spectra.md`](../ai/prompts/006-final-ps11-spectra.md)) sets out the changes. Angelyn's draft proposal for #11, "OrderPilot" (26 Oct 2026), was reviewed by the team against prompt 006, and prompt 007 ([`../ai/prompts/007-adopt-angelyn-orderpilot.md`](../ai/prompts/007-adopt-angelyn-orderpilot.md)) adopts its best points while 006 is carried out; where the two differ, 007 wins.

The record so far:

- **#7, LESSO Solar.** The platform was built and then retargeted to it, and the client withdrew on 23 Oct ([ADR 0022](0022-final-statement-7-lesso-solar.md)). The tag `archive/ps7-lesso-solar` keeps the repository as it stood.
- **#5, ASPRI-IPI.** Prompt 005 got through ADR 0023, the reframe, the assumptions and questions, the design-thinking set, the GRS design, the backlog and the GitHub forms (#148 to #182). Step 3 was open as #186, three of step 6's guides were on branches never opened as pull requests, and the #5 roles were on a local branch.
- **#11, Spectra Innovations.** A Singapore distributor, and master distributor for some brands, of IT products and computer peripherals, selling to South Asia and the Middle East on Microsoft Dynamics Navision. Its purchase and sales orders are handled by hand across six teams, and staff key the same data again from the purchase orders, proforma invoices, invoices, packing lists and shipping documents that customers and vendors send ([problem-statement.md](../problem-statement.md)).

Of everything built, #11 needs most of the platform: documents read into structured data through approved mappings, data checked across sources, approvals routed through a workflow engine, and every write to a system of record kept behind a person's approval. The question is what carries over, what changes, and in what order.

We will retarget the project to statement #11 by editing what exists, not by rebuilding it:

## Consequences

- **Positive.** The allocation is final, so the platform can settle. The brief's own principle, an AI that reads documents and a workflow engine that enforces the rules, matches what is built: approved mappings, preview before any write, the state machine, conflict detection and the audit trail. MVP first gives the W4 prototype and the W5 proposal one pilot that works end to end.
- **Negative.** The domain content needs rework a second time: master data, the mocks and the synthetic data, the screens, the screenshots and the deck. The two retargets cost the team most of a week. For several pull requests the documents describe #11 while the code still shows the HR domain.
- **Neutral.** The tags keep #7 and #5 reachable, so nothing built for them is lost.
